The cinema / outside class

The other
half of me.

Shows, anime, films, music, books, games and manga. Not a ranking. A trail of things that stayed with me.

Some of my first movies were CDs Dad brought home around 2010: Ghost Rider, TRON and Avatar. WALL·E, Toy Story and Cars were part of childhood too, but books were my favourite for years. I read over a hundred from ages 4 to 15—Diary of a Wimpy Kid, Tom Gates, Goosebumps, Enid Blyton mysteries, Percy Jackson, Harry Potter, The Maze Runner, Divergent, The Lord of the Rings, The Silent Patient and plenty more.

Then Interstellar blew me away in 2020. Two years later I went looking for it again, found a lot more films along the way, and got into TV shows. After Lucifer, Attack on Titan came up as a recommendation. That led to anime, then manga and manhwa, and eventually games. That’s roughly how this shelf happened.

The workshop / about & tools

Still figuring
it out.

Third-year MBA Tech Data Science student at NMIMS MPSTME, Mumbai. Currently learning Linux and cloud after finishing AWS Cloud Technical Essentials.

Building dashboards and APIs got me interested in the parts between them. Then I put a frontend, backend, database and Nginx together with Docker Compose, and started wondering what it takes to run the whole thing beyond my laptop.

That’s why I’m learning Linux and cloud now. I’m still early, but I like following a problem far enough to understand what actually fixed it.
