What stayed with me.

Nothing here is ranked. I just know I like these a lot.

01 / Watch

On the screen

Some are tense, some are playful, but they all make their worlds feel bigger than the screen. These notes stay clear of plot twists.

Series · Star Wars

Andor

The writing in this is some of the best Star Wars has ever had, right up there with Episode III and The Clone Wars for me. Cassian, Luthen, Bix, Mon Mothma and so many others believe in the rebellion while carrying their own darkness. Both seasons had me completely hooked. And knowing it connects to Rogue One, another peak film, makes it even better.

Miniseries · Drama

Chernobyl

Terrifying, but also deeply sad. Watching Valery Legasov and Boris Shcherbina understand what they were up against, then keep pushing through the government's denial, was haunting. They knew the cost and still fought to make the truth impossible to ignore. A series that leaves you quiet after it ends.

Animated miniseries

Over the Garden Wall

The art brought back that childhood feeling of getting lost in a cartoon, but the story is anything but childish. It's whimsical one minute and genuinely frightening the next. The journey, the strange world and the antagonist make it feel like an old tale you somehow remember from childhood. So good to experience.

Animated series

Arcane

The art style is beautiful, but the story is what kept me there. It's messy, unpredictable and full of characters making choices you understand even when they hurt. Almost every episode gave me something to think about or a scene I wanted to watch again. Definitely a must-watch.

Series · Marvel

Moon Knight

Oscar Isaac was so good in this. The shifts in identity made everything feel unpredictable, and the Egyptian mythology gave it an atmosphere I hadn't seen in a Marvel show before. It gets strange, dark and funny at different points, but somehow it all works. Really wanted more of this world.

02 / Watch

Movies

Some of these were first watches I can still place in a room and a year. Others were films I went into knowing almost nothing. All of them stuck.

Film · Science fiction

Interstellar

First watched this at home in 2020, during the Covid years, and it absolutely blew me away. The scale of space, the music, the way time and family pull at the story — everything felt massive and personal at once. The kind of film that makes you sit there when the credits roll. Still the goat for me.

Film · Racing drama

Ford v Ferrari

Matt Damon and Christian Bale were peak in this. The story of real people trying to build something extraordinary is already great, then the racing scenes make your heart pound. Even away from the track, the politics and the fight to be heard kept me hooked. The ending made it one of my favourites.

Film · Studio Ghibli

Spirited Away

My first Ghibli film, so it will always be close to my heart. The hand-drawn world feels alive in every corner, and the soundtrack makes it even harder to leave. Chihiro's journey and her growth made all that wonder feel personal. Absolutely fantastic from beginning to end.

Film · Drama

The Shawshank Redemption

Went into this expecting almost nothing, and Andy completely blew me away. The patience, the friendship, and that refusal to lose hope stay with you through the whole film. By the ending I had no words. One of those movies that makes you understand why people keep talking about it.

Film · Science fiction

Avatar

This is where it started. I was four when Dad brought a CD home in 2010 and played it on the TV, and Pandora left such a deep imprint that I still remember that first watch. The world is gorgeous, but the way people take land and resources from others mirrors the real world too. That part only hit harder as I grew up.

03 / Watch

Anime

From the first series that got me into anime to the quiet ones that left me speechless. These are the stories I kept thinking about long after an episode ended.

Anime series

Attack on Titan

My introduction to anime, and still one of the greatest things I've watched. So much about it would be ruined by explaining too much, but the way it changes your view of war stayed with me. What you believe can depend on which side of the wall you're standing on. Wow, what a series.

Anime series

Violet Evergarden

Episodes 9 and 10 had me emotional in a way I wasn't ready for. Violet's journey from being treated like a weapon to understanding herself as a person is so beautiful, especially as she helps others find the words they couldn't say. The art, the opening and ending songs, Evan Call's score — it all left me speechless.

Anime series

One Piece

The first time I started this, I was hooked. Luffy setting out from a small town, building the crew one person at a time, the fights, the humour, the emotional moments — somehow the journey keeps getting bigger without losing its heart. I watched so many episodes because I just wanted to see where they'd go next. Goated.

Anime series

Haikyu!!

Peak sports anime. Every player gets a reason to matter, the matches make a single point feel enormous, and the soundtrack knows exactly when to hit. Karasuno was always my team, though. Seeing Hinata keep chasing his dream despite his height made it impossible not to root for him.

Anime series

Code Geass

The politics, the sacrifices, the characters and the mecha — this had everything I wanted. Lelouch can be brilliant and infuriating in the same episode, which is why he's one of my favourite main characters ever. The story keeps making its choices count, and that ending was so good.

Anime series · Space opera

Legend of the Galactic Heroes

Still watching this, but every episode has me glued to the screen. The scale of the politics and the conflict is huge, yet the people on both sides are what make it so interesting. No one gets reduced to a simple side to cheer for. There's so much here to sit with.

Anime series · Drama

March Comes in Like a Lion

This was pure bliss, even when it hurt. The animation makes the quietest moments beautiful, and the characters have so much going on beneath what they say. Their growth took me through a whole whirlwind of emotions. By the end, masterpiece was the only word for it.

04 / Listen

Music

I usually notice melody first. Soundtracks and songs can bring an entire mood back in seconds, and an album can be its own place to spend time.

Song · Porter Robinson & Madeon

Shelter

The song is beautiful on its own, but the animated short gives it so much more weight. The music and visuals feel made for each other; one part always brings back the other. It's short, yet it leaves behind this huge feeling. Playing the track usually turns into watching the whole thing again.

Song · Casting Crowns

Who Am I

There's something so sincere about this song. The question in it sounds simple, but it gives me a lot to sit with every time. The way the vocals build without losing that sincerity is what makes it land. One of those songs that makes everything slow down for a minute.

Score · Evan Call

The Love That Binds Us

A few notes and Violet Evergarden is right there again. The score carries so much of the tenderness and sadness of Violet's journey without needing a single word. After seeing how much the series made me feel, this piece brings it all rushing back. Evan Call really did something special here.

Album · Daft Punk

Random Access Memories

This album has so much going on, and somehow it still feels effortless to listen to. The production, the little details, the way each song has its own identity — it makes me want to hear it from beginning to end. It feels warm and alive every time, which is why I keep returning to it.

Soundtrack album · RADWIMPS

Your Name

Lowkey one of the best and most emotional soundtracks I've heard. The songs are so tied to Your Name that a few notes can bring an entire scene and its feeling back. Even away from the film, the music is beautiful on its own. It really stays with you.

05 / Read

Books

I like having more time with a world and its people. Some of these I found through the films, then wanted to read the books too.

Book series · Suzanne Collins

The Hunger Games

The tension had me turning pages, but the series has a lot more on its mind than survival. The unfairness of its world, the pressure on the characters and the ways they try to protect each other all stayed with me. It's exciting to read, then you catch yourself thinking about it afterward.

Novel · Marcia Willett

A Week in Winter

A much quieter book than the others here, and that's exactly why it belongs. Spending time with the house and the people connected to it feels like slowly learning someone's history. Nothing needs to rush. It's the kind of story where settling into the lives of the characters is the whole pleasure.

Book series · C. S. Lewis

The Chronicles of Narnia

Stepping through something ordinary into an entire other world is a feeling that never gets old. Narnia has the wonder and adventure I want from a story like this, but it also has sadness and difficult choices. That mix gives the world weight. It feels like somewhere worth returning to.

Book trilogy · J. R. R. Tolkien

The Lord of the Rings

The films made me love Middle-earth, and the books let me stay there for so much longer. The scale of the journey is incredible, but the friendships are what really hold it together for me. All those small acts of courage matter just as much as the huge battles. Loved experiencing both versions.

Book series · James Dashner

The Maze Runner

Both the books and the movies were such a good time. Being thrown into a mystery with the characters and trying to make sense of each new piece had me hooked. There's always this urge to go one chapter or one film further just to get an answer. Really enjoyed that whole experience.

06 / Play

Games

I like games that let me sit with the characters and choices for a while, even after I'm done playing.

Visual novel

The House in Fata Morgana

This is so hard to talk about without giving anything away. The gothic atmosphere pulls you in, then the story keeps changing how you see its characters and everything around them. It asks you to stay with it, and the payoff makes that time feel worth it. Really difficult to forget afterward.

Narrative game · State of Play

South of the Circle

The art is gorgeous and almost calming, while the choices underneath it feel anything but easy. There's a quiet, human quality to the story that made its moments linger for me. It never needed to be loud to have an impact. By the end, I was still thinking about what those choices meant.

Narrative game · AdHoc Studio

Dispatch

The superhero setup is fun, but the team completely makes this game. The banter is great, everyone has a personality, and the choices mean more once you start caring about the people involved. It can be funny one minute and surprisingly sincere the next. That mix had me invested.

Choice-driven game · Quantic Dream

Detroit: Become Human

This game kept making me pause before choosing anything. Even a small decision felt like it could change where someone ended up, so it was hard to play without thinking about what I'd actually do. The different perspectives make the story more interesting too. Seeing the consequences of my choices was the best part.

Psychological horror · Wrong Organ

Mouthwashing

Genuinely unsettling in the best way a horror story can be. The atmosphere makes everything feel wrong before you can even put a reason to it, and the game doesn't rush to make you comfortable. Its characters and what's left unsaid sit in your head afterward. Not an easy experience, but a memorable one.

07 / Read

Manga & manhua

These are the ones I wanted to keep reading long after I should've gone to sleep.

Manga · Haruichi Furudate

Haikyu!!

Absolutely loved reading this. 'One more chapter' somehow turned into staying up until 4, and every rally made it harder to stop. It's not only Hinata and Karasuno either; the manga gives you reasons to care about so many people on the court. One of my favourite reading experiences, easily.

Manhua · Chan Mou

The Ravages of Time

The strategy in this is insane. Watching these characters try to outthink each other makes even a conversation feel like a battle, and one decision can stay in my head for ages. The Xiapi arc was peak for me. I was completely locked in and had to see where it went.

Manga · Kazuhiro Fujita

Karakuri Circus

The story goes through so much, but it's the characters who make you want to go through all of it with them. Their journeys, the connections between them, and then the way everything comes together at the end — biblical is honestly the word. That payoff still comes to mind.

Manga · Kiyohiko Azuma

Yotsuba&!

Such a wonderful slice of life. Yotsuba can turn an ordinary day into something funny, surprising or just really lovely to spend time with. Nothing huge has to happen for a chapter to work. That's what makes it so good: it gets you to pay attention to the little things too.

Manga · Asao Takamori & Tetsuya Chiba

Ashita no Joe

Peak main character, peak story, peak ending. Joe is so compelling through both the highs and the lows, and the story makes you feel the weight of everything he goes through. It earns its big moments instead of just handing them to you. That ending is staying with me for a long time.
